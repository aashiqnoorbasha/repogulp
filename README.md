# Nora

**Your health. Your habits. Your life.**

Nora is an AI-powered personal intelligence platform designed to help people better understand themselves, improve their well-being, and manage everyday life through intelligent, proactive assistance.

The long-term vision is to create a unified personal AI ecosystem that combines health intelligence, personalized insights, automation, and everyday assistance into one seamless experience.

---

## Vision

Nora aims to make managing personal well-being and everyday life significantly easier.

Rather than requiring users to constantly enter information, navigate dashboards, or ask questions, Nora is designed to work proactively within user-approved boundaries.

The goal is simple:

**Less manual input. Better understanding. More meaningful action.**

## Core Pillars

### 1. Nora Health

An intelligent health and wellness platform that brings personal information together and makes it easier to understand.

Planned capabilities include:

- Activity and movement tracking
- Sleep and recovery insights
- Nutrition and dietary information
- Wellness trends
- Personalized recommendations
- Health-data synchronization
- Long-term progress analysis

### 2. Nora Intelligence

The central intelligence layer powering the Nora ecosystem.

Its planned responsibilities include:

- Understanding personal context
- Analyzing information across connected sources
- Identifying meaningful patterns
- Generating personalized insights
- Providing contextual recommendations
- Supporting conversational assistance
- Proactive notifications and automation

### 3. Nora Assistant

A broader personal assistant designed to help users manage everyday activities.

Potential capabilities include:

- Personal organization
- Daily planning
- Reminders and routines
- Context-aware assistance
- Information management
- Intelligent task automation

## Platform Strategy

Nora is intended to support three platforms:

| Platform | Target |
|---|---|
| iOS | Native mobile experience |
| Android | Native mobile experience |
| Web | Full-featured browser experience |

The architecture should prioritize reusable business logic, shared backend services, and consistent experiences across platforms.

## Design Philosophy

Nora should feel modern, refined, intuitive, and effortless to use.

Design principles:

- Apple-inspired visual quality
- Thoughtful use of Liquid Glass and translucency
- Clear information hierarchy
- Smooth, purposeful animations
- Beautiful and readable data visualizations
- Responsive layouts
- Accessibility and usability
- Minimal friction throughout the experience

The interface should make complex information feel simple without sacrificing functionality.

## Intelligence & Automation

Nora's long-term intelligence architecture is intended to operate through several interconnected layers:

1. **Data Engine** — Collects, synchronizes, validates, and organizes information.
2. **Intelligence Engine** — Analyzes information and identifies patterns.
3. **Recommendation Engine** — Generates relevant, contextual suggestions.
4. **Conversational Intelligence** — Allows users to interact naturally with Nora.
5. **Proactive Intelligence** — Identifies opportunities to provide useful assistance.
6. **Agentic Capabilities** — Executes approved actions within defined permissions.

Automation must remain transparent, permission-based, and controllable by the user.

## Privacy & Trust

Personal information requires careful handling.

Nora's development must prioritize:

- Explicit user consent
- Secure authentication
- Permission-scoped integrations
- Protection of personal information
- Transparent data usage
- User-controlled automation
- Revocable permissions
- Clear distinction between measured, estimated, and unavailable information

Nora must never fabricate personal data or present unsupported conclusions as facts.

## Development Principles

Nora is being developed as an independent software project.

Development priorities:

- Maintainable architecture
- Modular and reusable components
- Reliable backend infrastructure
- Strong type safety where appropriate
- Consistent coding standards
- Automated testing
- Version control
- Cross-platform compatibility
- Documented integrations

AI-assisted development tools may be used to accelerate implementation, but all generated functionality must be reviewed and tested.
